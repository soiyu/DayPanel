# Google Play listing draft

App name: DayPanel: Habits & Daily Log

Short description: Track habits, amounts and counts. Quick widgets. Optional one-time Plus.

## Full description

Make room for the small things you want to keep doing.

DayPanel combines habits, everyday tasks and a personal journal. Check something off, record an amount such as pages or distance, or count how many times you practiced. Keep one-time tasks separate from reusable, as-needed tasks.

Use daily, weekday or interval schedules. See your history, take a break without losing your past, and keep notes alongside your habits. Your records stay on your device, and full file backup, restore and readable CSV export remain available without Plus.

Add a habit or task widget to your Android home screen. Mark eligible items done directly; numeric entries open an input so you can review the amount before saving. Widget sizing and background updates depend on your launcher and Android settings.

Optional DayPanel Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription:
- Combine selected habits and tasks in a single home-screen panel.
- Keep individual amount/count records with times and optional notes, and correct a single record.
- Set flexible weekly total goals, distinct from goals based on days completed.
- Make weekly or monthly review cards with selected content and a preview before saving or sharing.
- Choose extra original icons and coordinated color palettes.

Names and reflections are hidden in shared review images by default. Adding a reminder to your calendar opens an event draft for you to save; it is a separate copy, not automatic calendar synchronization.

No ads, no developer account and no cloud synchronization. A Google Play account is used for optional purchases and purchase restoration. Keep external backup files safe before changing phones or uninstalling. Earned daily cards keep their original snapshots when you later edit a habit.

# DayPanel Privacy Policy — publisher review draft

Status: prepare your publisher identity, contact email and effective date before publishing this at a public HTTPS address. This file is not an already-published policy.

DayPanel keeps habits, numeric/count logs, tasks, journals, earned cards and preferences in local app storage. It has no developer account, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics SDK. The developer does not receive these business records from normal use.

Optional Plus purchases are processed by Google Play. The app receives store purchase information and verifies/caches receipts to unlock and restore access. DayPanel does not receive payment card details. Google processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. Purchase receipts are not part of your DayPanel backup.

When you choose to back up/export/share, the selected file is handed to Android's document provider or sharing destination. When you choose Add to calendar, an event draft is handed to the calendar application for your review. These destinations can store or synchronize data according to their own policies. The app does not silently create calendar events or synchronize edits back from the calendar.

You control deletion in the app or through Android Clear storage/uninstall. A previous-data recovery copy is retained for rollback and replaced by the next restore. Earned cards can retain historical snapshots when a habit is deleted; clearing app storage removes local copies. External backups, shared images and calendar copies must be deleted separately. Deleting business data does not cancel a Google Play purchase; handle purchases through Play.

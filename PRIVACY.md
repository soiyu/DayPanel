# Privacy Policy for DayPanel

**Last updated:** November 14, 2026

**DayPanel** ("we", "our", or "the application") is a craftsman-built personal habit tracker, daily task organizer, and mindful journal for Android. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use DayPanel.

---

### 1. 100% Offline-First Architecture & No Data Collection

DayPanel is designed from the ground up as a **100% local, offline-first application**:
- **Local Storage Only**: All your habits, schedules, completion records, daily tasks, journal entries, earned achievement cards, and display preferences are stored exclusively on your local device in a private SQLite database.
- **No Developer Servers**: We do not operate developer accounts, user registration, authentication systems, or cloud databases. 
- **Zero Ads & Zero Telemetry**: DayPanel contains **no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no third-party analytics**. We do not harvest, inspect, monetize, or transmit your personal data or usage patterns.

---

### 2. Google Play In-App Purchases (DayPanel Plus)

DayPanel offers an optional one-time purchase ("DayPanel Plus") to unlock advanced features. 
- **Handled by Google Play**: All transactions and payments are securely processed directly by Google Play under [Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **No Financial Data Access**: The developer never accesses, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.
- **Local Receipt Caching**: The application receives a cryptographic purchase token from the official Google Play Billing library solely to activate and verify your Plus entitlements locally on your device. Purchase receipts are tied to your Google Play account and are excluded from local data backups.

---

### 3. Data Export, Backup, and Device Sharing

You have full control over your data at all times:
- **Backup & CSV Export**: When you create a JSON backup, export a CSV file, or save an achievement card image, the file is handed over to the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) or system share sheet upon your explicit action. DayPanel does not transmit these files across external networks.
- **Calendar Integration**: If you choose to add a reminder to your calendar, DayPanel opens a standard Android calendar event draft for you to review and confirm. It creates an independent calendar entry on your chosen calendar provider; it does not read, modify, or sync other personal calendar events.

---

### 4. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

- You can modify or delete habits, tasks, and journal entries at any time directly within the application.
- To permanently delete all application data, you can use the Android system settings (**Settings > Apps > DayPanel > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage**) or simply uninstall the application. 
- Uninstalling the application will remove the local database immediately. External backups and exported images saved outside the app directory must be managed separately by you.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

DayPanel is a general-purpose productivity and self-reflection utility intended for general audiences (ages 13 and older, and 18+ for financial transactions). It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
